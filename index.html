The Origin

Have you ever found yourself listening to your favorite playlist when, all of a sudden, you're transported to a state of nostalgia?

You're reliving a cherished memory, bookmarked in your brain by a song, but often forgotten until you hear it again.

That moment is special, hard to put into words, and short-lived.

So I'm building LifeTrack, as a place to store & share these memories.
